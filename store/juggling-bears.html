The Great Honeybridge Exhibition

An account of flying apples, airborne crockery, and one unfortunate pie.

The opening ceremony

On 14 June 1847, Barnaby Bear opened the exhibition with seven polished apples and a solemn bow. Permit JB-1847-001 allowed fruit, wooden clubs, and exactly one ceremonial teapot. The mayor requested that the teapot remain empty.

A most unusual rehearsal

On 16 June 1847, Beatrice Bear rehearsed beside the old clock tower. Under permit JB-1847-002, she juggled five silver spoons while Barnaby Bear balanced a honey jar upon his nose. The clerk recorded three encores and no missing spoons.

The incident of the blueberry pie

On 21 July 1848, Benedict Bear presented the guild's first edible finale. Permit JB-1848-003 specified six pies; the audience counted five. Beatrice Bear declined to explain the purple stain on her waistcoat. The performance was nevertheless declared a triumph.
